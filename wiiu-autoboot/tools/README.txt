wuhbtool packs the app and your game into a .wuhb. It is part of devkitPro's wut-tools.

Put the copy for your computer in this folder:

  Windows   wuhbtool.exe    (from a devkitPro install: C:\devkitPro\tools\bin\wuhbtool.exe)
  Linux     wuhbtool        (already here in the download; or /opt/devkitpro/tools/bin/wuhbtool)
  macOS     wuhbtool        (from a devkitPro install: /opt/devkitpro/tools/bin/wuhbtool)

If it is not here, the build script also looks on your PATH and in your devkitPro install.
